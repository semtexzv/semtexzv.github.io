Quick rules
  • White moves first. Men slide one square diagonally forward on the dark squares.
  • Capturing is mandatory: jump over an adjacent enemy piece into the empty square behind it. Keep jumping with the same piece as long as you can.
  • A man that ends its move on the far row becomes a king (dáma). Kings fly: they move and capture any distance along a diagonal.
  • You win by capturing or blocking all enemy pieces.
