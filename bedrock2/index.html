Every service, every agent, every rule is one build graph — including the runner that deploys it. Work arrives only as proposals: sandboxed, reviewed, recorded, upload-only; nothing merges itself. It writes, reviews, and ships its own software, then rewrites how it does all three — climbing, level by level, from a single hand-run seed toward a company that runs itself.
