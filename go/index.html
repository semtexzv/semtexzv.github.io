Quick rules
  • Take turns placing stones on the line crossings. Black goes first.
  • Surround an enemy group on all sides (no empty neighbors left) and you capture it.
  • You can't place a stone where it would instantly have no breathing room, and you can't immediately retake a single-stone capture (ko).
  • When neither of you wants to move, both pass. Then tap any hopeless "dead" stones to remove them, and confirm the score.
  • Score = your stones on the board + empty points only you surround. White gets 6.5 bonus points for going second.
